## 2026-04-24 - Missing required field indicators and aria attributes\n**Learning:** The forms in this app lack visual asterisk indicators and screen-reader `aria-required` attributes for mandatory inputs, leading to poor UX and accessibility issues when validation is tied directly to the form controls.\n**Action:** Whenever adding or modifying required forms, include `<span class="required-indicator" aria-hidden="true">*</span>` in labels and `aria-required="true"` on input elements.
